# CITIC Bank income-investment report

## 1. Executive summary

**Data cutoff: 2026-08-01.** This is a comparative income screen, not personalized investment advice. CITIC Bank H-share offers the highest verified/near-current headline yield in the set (5.61% at HK$7.81 on 2026-07-30; a separate nearest-close calculation was 5.73% at HK$7.45 on 2026-07-24), but its evidence is less complete and its capital/coverage trend is weaker than ICBC's. Its 2025 attributable-profit growth was 2.98%, ROE 9.39%, CET1 9.48%, NPL 1.15%, and coverage 203.61%; Q1 2026 CET1 fell to 9.33% and coverage to 202.45% while NIM eased to 1.61%.

Comparative snapshot: ICBC 4.71% yield, 0.64x PB, 6.54x PE, 2025 NPL 1.31%, coverage 213.6%, capital adequacy 18.76%; CCB 4.82%, 0.58x PB, 6.23x PE; CMB 4.46%, 0.98x PB, 7.20x PE, NPL 0.94%, NIM 1.87%, coverage 391.79%, ROAE 13.44%.

**Analytical judgment:** CITIC can be a small opportunistic-income candidate, not a core-income conviction on this evidence. CMB is the quality/book-value-growth leader; ICBC is the stronger large-bank income benchmark; CCB is a reasonable value/income alternative.

## 4. Business and source of distributed cash

**Verified fact:** CITIC is a regulated commercial bank; supplied evidence reports attributable profit of RMB70.618bn in 2025 and RMB20.098bn in Q1 2026. Dividends therefore depend on recurring bank earnings, regulatory capital, and board payout decisions, not industrial free cash flow.

**Unknown:** cash-flow-after-investment, distributable earnings reconciliation, buyback competition, debt maturities, and off-balance-sheet commitments were not supplied. Do not interpret the PE-implied payout as a bank regulatory payout ratio.

## 6. Distribution coverage and safety

**Analytical proxy only:** dividend yield x PE gives a payout proxy of 35.23% at the 6.28x PE/5.61% yield snapshot, leaving 64.77% as an earnings-retention proxy. This is not proof of distributable-earnings coverage.

**Verified facts:** 2025 NPL 1.15%, special-mention 1.62%, overdue 1.43%, provision coverage 203.61%, CET1 9.48%; Q1 2026 NPL 1.15%, special-mention 1.63%, coverage 202.45%, CET1 9.33%. These are adequate but not strengthening indicators.

## 7. Balance sheet and refinancing

**Verified fact:** Q1 2026 group CET1 declined 15 bps from 2025 year-end; annualized ROE was 11.11% versus 11.42% a year earlier. NPL balance rose to RMB68.745bn. No maturity ladder or refinancing stress data were supplied. For a bank, capital depletion is the key dividend constraint; monitor CET1 relative to the bank's management buffer and regulatory minimum.

## 8. Income growth

2025 attributable profit grew 2.98%; Q1 2026 grew 3.02% YoY and revenue grew 5.23%. NIM declined 1.63% to 1.61%, and annualized ROE declined 11.42% to 11.11%. This supports modest book-value growth, but not a high-confidence growth thesis.

For an illustrative comparison, using ROE as a book-value-growth proxy and the yield x PE payout proxy: CITIC implied retained-growth proxy is about **6.08%**, versus ICBC about **6.77%**, CCB about **6.51%**, and CMB about **9.12%**. These are analytical estimates, not reported book-value growth.

**Precise condition under which CITIC beats ICBC:** holding the observed yields and PEs constant, CITIC's sustainable ROE must exceed **9.07%** (assuming the same payout-proxy method). More robustly, CITIC beats ICBC only if its future recurring ROE is above 9.07% **and** CET1 remains at least stable (not continuing the 9.48% to 9.33% decline) while dividend per share is maintained or increased. Reported 2025 ROE of 9.39% clears the mechanical threshold narrowly, but the capital trend prevents treating that as established superiority.

## 9. Valuation and margin of safety

CITIC's 6.28x PE and 0.49x PB are cheaper than CMB's 7.20x/0.98x and similar in PE to CCB's 6.23x/0.58x; ICBC is 6.54x/0.64x. CITIC's yield premium to ICBC is about 0.90 percentage points, but the exact 2026-07-31 price and yield are not verified. The discount is a potential margin of safety only if capital quality stabilizes; otherwise it may price weaker book-value compounding.

## 13. Dividend-cut risks

Primary risks are continued CET1 erosion, weaker provision coverage, higher early-warning loans, NIM compression, and a policy decision to retain earnings. The incomplete five-year distribution record is itself a risk to durability assessment. A high displayed yield caused by a falling share price would be a yield-trap signal, not a bullish signal.

## 14. Purchase or reinforcement conditions

Study purchase only if: (1) official filings verify at least five years of maintained ordinary dividends; (2) CET1 stabilizes or improves for two reporting periods; (3) coverage stabilizes above roughly 200% and special-mention/NPL trends stop worsening; (4) recurring ROE remains above **9.07%** and preferably exceeds ICBC after adjusting for risk; (5) the yield premium to ICBC remains at least 0.75 percentage points without a corresponding deterioration in credit metrics; and (6) the exact price, dividend, and withholding treatment are independently verified.

## 15. Reduction or sale conditions

Reduce or reject an addition if CET1 continues falling, coverage falls materially below 200%, special-mention or NPL ratios rise persistently, recurring ROE drops below 9.07%, the ordinary dividend is cut, or the yield premium is explained by deteriorating earnings rather than undervaluation. Exit analysis must also consider currency and portfolio concentration.
